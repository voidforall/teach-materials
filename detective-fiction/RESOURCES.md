# Resources — detective fiction

## High-trust references
- [Britannica — detective fiction](https://www.britannica.com/art/detective-story) — compact genre history from Poe to the present.
- [Wikipedia — History of crime fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_crime_fiction) — detailed, well-cited timeline by era and country.
- [Wikipedia — Golden Age of Detective Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Age_of_Detective_Fiction) — the 1920s–40s puzzle era, Detection Club, Knox/Van Dine rules.
- [Wikipedia — Honkaku mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honkaku_mystery) / [Shin honkaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin_honkaku) — Japanese orthodox and new-orthodox schools.
- [Knox's Decalogue (1989 reprint)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Knox#Knox%27s_Decalogue) and [S. S. Van Dine's twenty rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Van_Dine#Van_Dine.27s_rules) — the fair-play canon.
- [The Detection Club](https://www.detectionclub.com/) — the Golden Age authors' society, still active.
- [Mystery Writers of America](https://www.mysterywriters.org/) — Edgar Awards; a live index of the genre's canon.
- [Encyclopedia.com — hard-boiled fiction](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/culture-magazines/hard-boiled-fiction) — Black Mask lineage, Hammett/Chandler/Macdonald.

## Reading-list anchors (spoiler-safe)
- [Goodreads — Best Mystery & Detective lists](https://www.goodreads.com/list/tag/mystery) — crowd-ranked, useful for sizing reputations.
- [The Guardian — the best crime fiction](https://www.theguardian.com/books/series/crime-fiction) — review-driven, UK-leaning.
- [Publishers Weekly reviews](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/genres/crime.html) — pre-publication, spoiler-free by convention.
