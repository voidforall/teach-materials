Valuation Basics — Cheat Sheet

The 10-lesson Damodaran-based course, compressed to one printable page: TVM, the DCF skeleton, cash flows, discount rates, growth, terminal value, multiples, biases.

1 · Price vs value — Lesson 1

  • Value = f(cash flows, growth, risk). Price = what the market pays. Know which you're producing.
  • Three approaches: intrinsic (DCF), relative (multiples = pricing), contingent claim (options).
  • Every valuation is a bridge between a story and numbers; each disciplines the other.

2 · The four TVM blocks — Lesson 2

BlockFormulaUse
Lump sumPV = CFn/(1+r)nAny single payment
AnnuityPV = CF·[1−(1+r)−n]/rLevel payments, n periods
Growing annuityPV = CF1/(r−g)·[1−((1+g)/(1+r))n]Forecast window
Growing perpetuityPV = CF1/(r−g), g<rTerminal value

Never mix nominal rates with real cash flows. A DCF = growing annuity + discounted growing perpetuity.

3 · The DCF skeleton — Lesson 3

Equity DCFFirm DCF
Cash flowFCFE (after debt payments)FCFF (before debt)
RateCost of equityWACC
OutputEquity valueFirm value − net debt = equity

Claim-match always. Banks → equity DCF (debt is raw material).

4 · Cash flows — Lesson 4

FCFF = EBIT(1−t) − (capex − depreciation) − ΔWC
FCFE = Net income + Depr − capex − ΔWC − (new debt − repaid)
  • Normalize one-time items; capitalize leases; treat R&D as investment (amortize).
  • FCF = earnings − reinvestment. Read the reason for low FCF before judging it.

5 · Discount rates — Lessons 5–6

Cost of equity = rf + β × ERP           (rf: currency- & horizon-matched govt bond, default-cleansed)
Cost of debt   = (rf + default spread) × (1−t)
WACC = E/(D+E)·ke + D/(D+E)·kd(1−t)     (market values, target mix)
β_L = β_U × (1 + (1−t)·D/E)            (bottom-up industry beta, relevered)
  • ERP: prefer implied (inverted DCF on the index) ≈ 4–5% US in recent years; Damodaran's datasets updated annually.
  • Add country risk premium for risky-currency/country cash flows.

6 · Growth — Lesson 7

g = reinvestment rate × ROC          (equity version: g = retention × ROE)
required reinvestment = g / ROC
  • Growth is paid for with reinvestment; quality of growth = ROC vs cost of capital.
  • Long-run g ≤ risk-free rate ≈ nominal GDP. Nothing outgrows the economy forever.

7 · Terminal value — Lesson 8

TV_n = FCFF_{n+1} / (WACC_stable − g)
  • Terminal firm must be stable: g ≤ rf, β→1, mature debt ratio, reinvestment = g/ROC.
  • Let excess returns fade unless a real moat; exit multiple = pricing, cross-check only.
  • TV is usually 60–80% of value — sensitivity live there.

8 · Multiples — Lesson 9

P/E = payout(1+g)/(ke − g)           EV/EBITDA ≈ f(ROC, g, t, WACC)
  • Equity multiples ↔ equity claims; firm multiples ↔ EV. Never cross (EV/net income!).
  • A multiple is a compressed DCF: cheap/expensive only relative to growth, returns, risk.
  • Four checks: consistent, uniform, explained by fundamentals, distribution (median > mean; losses vanish from P/E screens).

9 · Bias audit — Lesson 10

  • Anchoring: write story & assumptions before checking the price.
  • Confirmation: revalue under the opposite thesis.
  • Certainty: deliver a three-scenario range, not a point.
  • Output = clarified story + named bet, never "the market is irrational."

The one-line model

Value of firm = Σ FCFF_t/(1+WACC)^t  +  TV_n/(1+WACC)^n
Equity value   = firm value − (debt − cash);  per share = /shares
Sanity scan:   g = reinvestment × ROC at every stage; terminal g ≤ rf
