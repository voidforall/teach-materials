# Valuation Basics Resources

## Knowledge

- [Damodaran on Valuation — NYU Stern course hub](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/)
  Home of everything Damodaran: lecture webcasts, lecture notes (PDF), spreadsheets, datasets. The single most-trusted source for this course.
- [Damodaran: Valuation class lecture notes & webcasts](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalspr24.htm)
  Full semester of recorded lectures (Spring 2024). Sessions 1–10 cover everything in this course. Use for: every lesson's primary source.
- [Investment Valuation (3rd ed.), Aswath Damodaran](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/PdFiles/valn2ed.pdf) — the encyclopedic text (older edition is free online; latest edition in print). Use for: deep dives on each lesson topic.
- [The Little Book of Valuation, Aswath Damodaran](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/PdFiles/littlebook.pdf) — the short, practical version. Use for: first-pass reading, DCF-in-one-page.
- [Damodaran's annual datasets (equity risk premiums, betas by sector, margins)](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datacurrent.html)
  Updated every January: implied ERPs, industry fundamentals. Use for: lessons 5–9 exercises with real numbers.
- [The Equity Risk Premium (Damodaran, annual update paper)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4208994) — the definitive survey on estimating ERP. Use for: Lesson 5.
- [Musings on Markets (Damodaran blog)](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com/)
  Valuations of real companies (Tesla, Apple, Nvidia…) done in public. Use for: capstone wisdom, seeing the craft applied.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/finance / r/investing valuation threads](https://www.reddit.com/r/investing/) — amateur-practitioner debates; good for testing your own reasoning.
- [Damodaran's "Data Updates" posts comments & r/ValueInvesting](https://www.reddit.com/r/ValueInvesting/) — where DCFs get picked apart by practitioners. Use for: wisdom lessons.
- [Wisesheets / Valeía style valuation communities] — skip; prefer the blog comments and Reddit threads attached to each Damodaran valuation post.
