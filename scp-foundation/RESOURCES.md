# SCP Foundation Resources

## Knowledge

- [Object Classes — SCP Wiki essay](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/object-classes)
  Canonical definitions of every object class and the core "containment difficulty, not danger" principle. Use for: anything about how anomalies are classified. *(Grounds Lesson 1.)*
- [Anomaly Classification System (ACS) Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/anomaly-classification-system-guide)
  The newer optional "danger diamond" rating bar (Containment / Disruption / Risk). Use for: understanding modern articles that show a colored ACS header.
- [SCP Series Archive — Series I onward](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-series)
  The master index of all SCP articles by number. Use for: locating any SCP-#### and browsing.
- [Top Rated Pages](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/top-rated-pages)
  Community-upvoted best-of. Use for: finding the genuinely great articles instead of drowning in the archive. *(Curation shortcut for the mission.)*
- [Groups of Interest hub](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/groups-of-interest)
  The rival/allied organisations of the setting (GoIs). Use for: the planned "Groups of Interest" lesson.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/SCP](https://www.reddit.com/r/SCP/)
  Large, active reader community — recommendations, lore discussion, "explain this article" threads. Use for: finding what to read next and sanity-checking interpretations.
- [Official SCP Wiki forums](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/forum/start)
  Where authors and long-time readers discuss articles and canon. Use for: deep-lore questions and author intent.
