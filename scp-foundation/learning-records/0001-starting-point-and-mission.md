# Starting point: casual reader, reading-for-appreciation mission

Established at course start (2026-07-11). The learner is a **casual SCP reader**: knows the famous entries (SCP-173, 096, 682) and the general clinical-horror vibe, but not the deeper canon or the writing craft. Mission is **reading appreciation**, not writing or building — see [[MISSION.md]].

**Implications for future sessions:**
- Teach the *reading lens* — how to extract more from an article — before any deep-canon completism.
- Assume the famous entries are already known; use them as familiar anchors, don't re-explain them.
- Zone of proximal development after Lesson 1 (Object Class Lens): the recurring *structural pillars* of the world — Groups of Interest, MTFs / O5, and how to navigate 8,000+ articles to the good ones. Article structure/anatomy and the "less is more" redaction craft are also strong candidates.
- Not yet demonstrated understanding — Lesson 1 material is *covered*, not yet *retrieved under test*. Watch for evidence (a correct object-class call on an unfamiliar article) before treating it as learned.
