# Mission: SCP Foundation — Reading the Lore

## Why
I want to read and *appreciate* the SCP Foundation deeply — not just recognise the famous entries, but understand the world well enough that any article, canon, or deep-lore reference lands with full weight. I read it for enjoyment; I want that enjoyment to get richer, not stay at meme-level.

## Success looks like
- I can open any SCP article and correctly read its object class, structure, and what it's *not* saying (redactions, implication).
- I know the major recurring pillars — Groups of Interest, key MTFs, the O5 Council, foundational canons — and recognise them when they appear.
- I can explain to a friend *why* a beloved SCP is beloved (the craft move that makes it work), not just what it does.
- I know how to find the genuinely great articles instead of drowning in 8,000+ entries.

## Constraints
- Learning for pleasure, not a deadline. Short, self-contained lessons I can do in a sitting.
- Casual-reader starting point: knows SCP-173 / 096 / 682 and the general vibe; not the deeper canon or the writing craft.
- Prefer beautiful, re-readable reference docs (I'll come back to them).

## Out of scope (for now)
- *Writing* my own SCP articles / passing wiki peer review — reading appreciation first.
- Building a game/app in the universe.
- Exhaustive completism (memorising every SCP). Depth on the load-bearing pieces, not coverage.
