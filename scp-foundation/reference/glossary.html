Glossary & Object Classes

The compressed reference. The one place terms are defined — every lesson defers here.

Object Classes

Class = containment difficulty, never danger. Source: Object Classes essay.

Primary

Safe
Reliably contained with modest effort. Often inert until triggered. Lethal-but-Safe is common.
Euclid
Containable but unreliable; incomplete understanding or unpredictability. Autonomous / alive / sapient defaults here.
Keter
Exceedingly hard to contain; elaborate, breach-prone procedures. Not a synonym for "evil".
Thaumiel
Used by the Foundation to contain other anomalies. Existence highly classified (O5 only).

Secondary & escalation

Apollyon
Cannot be contained / breaching imminently / world-ending. The "the end is here" class.
Archon
Containable in theory, but deliberately left uncontained — containing it would do more harm.
Cernunnos
Functionally containable, but the Foundation chooses not to (logistical / ethical reasons).
Ticonderoga
Uncontainable but needs no containment — ubiquitous on Earth or off-world.

Disposition (status, not difficulty)

Neutralized
No longer anomalous — destroyed, disabled, or properties ceased (not necessarily on purpose).
Decommissioned
Deliberately destroyed / stripped of anomaly by the Foundation. Intent is the difference from Neutralized.
Explained
Turned out to be mundane science, a hoax, or a misunderstanding.
Pending / Uncontained
Not yet classed (too little data) / not yet contained (effort ongoing).
Esoteric
Story-specific one-off classes. Officially recognised only if reused across 25+ articles.

Core vocabulary

The Foundation — the secret organisation that Secures, Contains, and Protects anomalies to keep normalcy intact ("Secure, Contain, Protect").

SCP-#### — an anomalous object, entity, or phenomenon, and by extension the article documenting it. Numbers are roughly chronological, not ranked.

Containment Procedures — the "how we keep it in the box" section; usually the first thing in an article.

O5 Council — the thirteen highest-authority overseers. Maximum clearance; identities secret.

MTF — Mobile Task Force: specialist teams deployed to recover or re-contain anomalies (e.g. MTF Epsilon-11, "Nine-Tailed Fox").

GoI — Group of Interest: any organisation involved in the paranormal world (corp, cult, government, collective). File each by what it wants an anomaly for.

Special Containment Procedures — the "how we keep it in the box" section; appears before the Description to build dread. Should never be redacted. See Lesson 3.

Description — what the anomaly actually is; starts mundane, turns anomalous. The reveal, after the procedures.

Addendum — appended material (experiment logs, interviews, updates) after the main file; usually where the narrative arc / twist lives.

Clinical tone — the flat scientific-report voice all articles use. The gap between dispassionate voice and horrific content is the core dread device.

ACS — Anomaly Classification System: a newer, optional "danger diamond" bar that adds finer risk/containment ratings alongside the traditional class.

Redaction / [DATA EXPUNGED] — deliberately hidden text. The horror lives in the gap; the reader's imagination fills it. A core stylistic device.

Tale — narrative fiction set in the universe, without the clinical anomaly format. Characters and plot. See Tales Hub.

Canon — a curated, opt-in shared universe multiple authors build on with internal consistency. See Canon Hub and Lesson 5.

"There is no canon" — the site's founding principle. Not zero canon but a multitude that overlap; no single "true version". When articles contradict, neither is wrong. Ask what the author is doing, not which is real.

Series — SCP numbers grouped in blocks of 1000 (Series I = 001–999, II = 1000–1999…). Chronological, not quality-ranked — low numbers are the oldest.

Top Rated Pages — community-upvoted best-of list; the recommended front door for finding great articles instead of reading by number.

SCP-001 — a reserved slot holding multiple competing "proposals" (different authors' takes on the first anomaly). Cryptic; not a newcomer starting point.

Groups of Interest (by motive)

Motive → stance toward the Foundation. Source: GoI hub. See Lesson 2.

Chaos Insurgency
Power. Ex-Foundation defectors out to destroy it — the mirror enemy.
Global Occult Coalition
A clean world. UN-linked paranormal police who destroy anomalies — rival cop.
Church of the Broken God
Divine machinery. Metal religion rebuilding a machine god — adversary. (Pairs with Sarkic.)
Sarkic Cults (Nälkä)
Mastery of flesh. Organic-manipulation cannibal cults — adversary, XK-capable. (Flesh vs. the Church's metal.)
Marshall, Carter & Dark
Profit. Broker selling anomalies to the ultra-rich — amoral rival.
Are We Cool Yet?
To be seen. "Anartists" making fatal anomalous art — anti-Veil, chaotic.
The Serpent's Hand
Liberation. Frees sentient anomalies from containment — the moral counter-argument.
