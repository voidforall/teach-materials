Market Making Cheat Sheet

The whole course on one page: the P&L identity, the formulas, the reaction ladder, the risk checks, the architecture.

The one identity

P&L ≈ Σ spread − Σ adverse selection − Σ inventory cost − fees

Every bot knob maps to one term. Spread must scale with volatility; fills are negatively selected; inventory cost ≈ ½γσ²q².

Avellaneda–Stoikov, compressed

  • Reference: r = s − qγσ²(T−t) — skew against inventory, harder when volatile or near end of session.
  • Total spread: γσ²(T−t) + (2/γ)ln(1+γ/k) — vol term + fill-decay (k) term; post half each side of r.
  • γ, k are tuning knobs: calibrate k from fill-rate vs distance; set γ to tolerable skew, then halve.

Quoting policies

  • Skew: bid/ask = ref ∓ half_spread + (q/q_max)·max_skew. Soft shaping.
  • Ladder: layered sizes at increasing distances; mean-reversion capture; needs trend filter + hard cap.
  • Hard limit: at q_max stop quoting the adding side — enforced in the risk module, not the strategy.

Toxicity ladder (escalate in order)

Widen → Fade (move reference with flow) → Reduce size → Pull quotes. Signals: post-fill drift vs half-spread, rolling flow imbalance, VPIN, thin+wide inside spread. Around scheduled news: no quotes at all.

Risk pre-flight checklist

  • Position limit per symbol, in value-at-risk, outside strategy code.
  • Daily loss limit → cancel-all + flatten + stop for the day (not "reduce size").
  • Kill switch: manual + auto (stale feed, error spike, impossible P&L rate).
  • Portfolio/factor exposure cap across correlated symbols.
  • Size from stressed σ; survive a gap on max inventory.

Bot pipeline

exchange → market data (mid/micro/σ) → strategy (pure fn: state→quotes) → risk gate → gateway → exchange, with telemetry on every decision.

  • Fill model honesty: fill on trade-through, discount for queue.
  • Gateway owns position truth; reconcile every fill + periodically.
  • Promotion ladder: replay backtest → paper (weeks) → minimum real size.
