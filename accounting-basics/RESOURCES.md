# Accounting Basics Resources

## Knowledge

- [SEC investor.gov — Beginner's Guide to Financial Statements](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/how-read-10-k/how-read-10-k)
  The regulator's own plain-English walkthrough of the three statements inside a 10-K. Use for: Lessons 1, 2, 8 primary source.
- [Warren Buffett, Letters to Shareholders](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html)
  The best writing anywhere on earnings quality, accounting distortions, and owner earnings. Use for: Lessons 5, 7; wisdom throughout.
- [Aswath Damodaran — NYU Stern course hub](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/)
  His corporate-finance and valuation lecture notes open with statement/depreciation/cash-flow primers written for non-accountants. Use for: Lessons 2–6 grounding.
- [Howard Schilit et al., *Financial Shenanigans*](https://www.amazon.com/Financial-Shenanigans-Detecting-Accounting-Gimmicks/dp/0071703071) — the catalog of earnings-manipulation schemes. Use for: Lesson 7.
- [Stephen Penman, *Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation*](https://www.amazon.com/Financial-Statement-Analysis-Security-Valuation/dp/0073379505) — the deep text on how statements articulate. Use for: going deeper after the course.
- [Khan Academy — Finance & Capital Markets: Financial Statements](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/core-finance/financial-states-info) — clear worked examples of the three statements. Use for: worked examples when stuck.
- [SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=) / [company filings](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany) — the primary documents themselves. Use for: Lesson 8 and capstone exercises.
- [CFI — Free Financial Modeling & Accounting Guides](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/) — quick reference on individual line items (D&A, deferred revenue, stock comp). Use for: glossary lookups.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ValueInvesting](https://www.reddit.com/r/ValueInvesting/) — statement teardowns of real companies; good place to test your reading.
- [r/Accounting](https://www.reddit.com/r/Accounting/) — practitioners who explain the "why" behind line items.
- [Damodaran's Musings on Markets comments](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com/) — valuations done in public, always starting from the statements.
