# Mission: Adlerian Psychology — Ideas and a Way of Living

## Why
The user wants to understand Alfred Adler's individual psychology — its core ideas (teleology, inferiority feelings, lifestyle, social interest, the life tasks) — and, more importantly, what it prescribes: what life attitude and values a person should adopt according to Adler. This is practical philosophy as much as psychology: the goal is a usable outlook on life, not academic history.

## Success looks like
- Explain Adler's break with Freud: etiology (causes in the past) vs teleology (goals in the present).
- Explain inferiority feelings, compensation, and the striving for superiority — as an engine, not a wound.
- Define lifestyle (Lebensstil) and Gemeinschaftsgefühl (social interest / community feeling).
- Name the three life tasks (work, friendship, love) and apply separation of tasks to real dilemmas.
- Distinguish vertical from horizontal relationships, and encouragement from praise.
- Articulate the Adlerian life attitude: self-acceptance, contribution over recognition, courage to be ordinary, and living in the present moment — and translate it into daily practices and personal values.

## Constraints
- Bilingual: every lesson ships as `NNNN-slug.html` + `NNNN-slug.zh.html`.
- Audience: an intelligent general reader; ground ideas in concrete everyday situations, citation-backed (Britannica, Wikipedia, Adlerian organizations, *The Courage to Be Disliked* where apt).
- Short lessons, each with a retrieval quiz (equal-length answers) and a small real-life practice task.

## Out of scope
- Clinical Adlerian therapy protocols; deep academic Adler scholarship; Freud–Jung–Adler biography beyond what illuminates the ideas.
