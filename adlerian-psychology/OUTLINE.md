# Course outline — 10 lessons

1. **The Third Viennese School: Adler's Break with Freud** — who Adler was; etiology vs teleology; "no experience determines your life, only the meaning you give it"; symptoms serve goals.
2. **The Inferiority Engine** — inferiority feelings (Minderwertigkeitsgefühl) as universal and useful; compensation and the striving for superiority; inferiority *complex* as an excuse; healthy vs neurotic striving.
3. **Lifestyle: The Chosen Self** — Lebensstil as a personal grammar of goal, self-image, and worldview; formed early but revisable; "creative self"; you are not your past.
4. **Social Interest: Gemeinschaftsgefühl** — community feeling as the compass of mental health; the three life problems as social relations; cooperation over competition.
5. **The Three Life Tasks** — work, friendship, love; each as a relation of cooperation; avoidance through "escape patterns"; courage as the answer.
6. **Separation of Tasks** — whose task is this?; you cannot make others change; freedom from recognition-seeking; "not living others' lives."
7. **Horizontal vs Vertical Relationships** — praise and rebuke as manipulation from above; equality of all "in the same here and now"; encouragement, gratitude, and respect instead.
8. **Self-Acceptance and the Courage to Be Ordinary** — accepting the irreplaceable this-self; comparison and envy as vertical traps; the courage to be normal; "guiding star" over perfection.
9. **Living in Moments: Happiness as Contribution** — dynamism vs trajectory; life as a dance, not a journey to a summit; happiness = feeling of contribution; existential meaning is given by living, not found.
10. **Capstone: The Adlerian Life Attitude** — synthesizing the course into a personal values charter: teleological self-understanding, task separation, horizontal relations, contribution, present-moment living; a 7-day practice plan.
