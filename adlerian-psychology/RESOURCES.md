# Resources — Adlerian psychology

## High-trust references
- [Britannica — Alfred Adler](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Alfred-Adler) — compact authoritative biography and summary of individual psychology.
- [Wikipedia — Alfred Adler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Adler) — well-cited overview of life, theory, and legacy.
- [Wikipedia — Individual psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual_psychology) / [Inferiority complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inferiority_complex) / [Style of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style_of_life) — the core concepts, cited.
- [North American Society of Adlerian Psychology (NASAP)](https://alsadler.org/) — the professional Adlerian association.
- [ICASSI — International Committee of Adlerian Summer Schools](https://www.icassi.com/) — living Adlerian education.
- [Adler Graduate School resources](https://alfredadler.edu/) — modern Adlerian theory and practice.

## Primary texts (course canon)
- Alfred Adler, *Understanding Human Nature* (1927) / *What Life Should Mean to You* (1931) / *The Practice and Theory of Individual Psychology* (1920s essays).
- Rudolf Dreikurs, *Social Equality: The Challenge of Today*; his children's-education work (*Children: The Challenge*).
- Ichiro Kishimi & Fumitake Koga, *The Courage to Be Disliked* (2013, EN 2018) — the Socratic dialogue that made Adler a global bestseller; heavily informs this course's "life attitude" lessons.
